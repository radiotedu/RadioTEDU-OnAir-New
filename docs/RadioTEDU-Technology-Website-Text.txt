RECOMMENDED WEBSITE TEXT

RadioTEDU streams professionally managed digital audio measured with the ITU-R BS.1770-5 loudness and true-peak algorithms and aligned with the EBU R128 broadcast reference. Our internally compiled, non-distributed FFmpeg build uses Fraunhofer FDK AAC: HE-AAC v2 for efficient low-bitrate listening and AAC-LC at 192 kbit/s for our full-quality stream. RadioTEDU Classical and RadioTEDU Jazz are also available as lossless FLAC streams. See our Technology page for more.
